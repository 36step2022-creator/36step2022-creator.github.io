Privacy policy — AI Jack (personal)

Last updated: 4 November 2026

AI Jack is a personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. It has no other users.

Data accessed

  • Gmail — read-only (gmail.readonly): message metadata and content, to summarise important letters for the owner.
  • Google Calendar — events (calendar.events): to show the owner's agenda and, only after the owner confirms, create or change the owner's own events.
  • Google Docs and Sheets — read-only (documents.readonly, spreadsheets.readonly): documents the owner asks about.
  • Google Drive — only files created by the app (drive.file).

How data is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer and shown only to the owner in the owner's private Telegram chat. Access tokens are stored locally with owner-only file permissions. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalised AI models. Short excerpts may be sent to the AI model provider the owner uses, solely to produce the owner's summaries.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local data and tokens.

Contact

The app owner, via the support e-mail shown on the Google consent screen.
